The dashboard adopts Rustrak's design system: a new app shell with sidebar, topbar and breadcrumbs, Inter and Geist Mono with Hugeicons, a restyled login, and every screen rebuilt on the shared tokens. Settings get their own sidebar mode; Appearance becomes Preferences (language, time zone, currency, theme), with old `/settings/appearance` links redirecting, and changing your password moves to a dialog in Profile. List pages gain a query bar beside the filter pills, including "is not" filters. The bundled price list is refreshed.
